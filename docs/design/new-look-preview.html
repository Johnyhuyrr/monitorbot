Two things worth knowing before this goes live.

The faces in this mock-up are stand-ins. In the real bot the picture is downloaded from that handle's public profile the moment the button is pressed, so every job shows the actual person you are working for.

A handle that does not answer is written as Not reachable, and a rate-limited check as Could not check. Neither is ever written as a ban — Instagram gives the identical answer for a delete, a deactivation and a rename.

Your logo is used exactly as you sent it. The only change is a light passing across it once, then a pause before it repeats.
